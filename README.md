We Mental Health
A website supporting the mental health and wellbeing of female athletes 
built with a focus on women and girls in sport across Africa and Nigeria,
alongside a growing international audience.
Mental health is treated as seriously as physical health here. The site
gives athletes real information, a confidential way to reach out about
what they're going through, and a path to real local help not just
another static page of advice.
What's on the site
Home, About, Mission, Resources, and Contact, all in one page with
a left-side tab navigation (no reloads between sections)
Articles (articles.html) general mental health awareness in
women's sport
Stress and Anxiety Management Tips (stress.html)
Understanding Anxiety (anxiety.html)
Self-Care and Mindfulness Practices (selfcare.html)
Postpartum Depression (postpartum.html)
Sex Education for Female Athletes (sexed.html) body literacy,
consent, and recognizing power imbalances in coach/athlete relationships
Community Support (community.html) a confidential form covering
sexual abuse, physical abuse, anxiety, pressure, depression, personal/
family issues, and career decisions, plus a live, country-based crisis
resource directory (currently Nigeria and Spain, with a global fallback)
